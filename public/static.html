About Us

We have developed an artificial intelligence chatbot that analyzes ocular fundus images to detect and classify various eye diseases, including glaucoma and cataract, and so on. Our system is designed to improve early diagnosis, enhance patient care and support telemedicine by providing fast and accurate analysis.

Learn More
Normal (N)

Normal ocular fundus with no evidence of pathology or abnormality.
Diabetes (D)

Damage to retinal blood vessels caused by prolonged high blood sugar levels.
Glaucoma (G)

A disease characterized by increased intraocular pressure and damage to the optic nerve, which can lead to vision loss.

Cataract (C)

A clouding of the lens of the eye that causes vision impairment and is often associated with age-related changes.
Age-Related Macular Degeneration (A)

Age-related macular degeneration leading to loss of central vision and retinal damage.
Hypertension (H)

Hypertensive retinopathy is damage to the blood vessels in the retina due to high blood pressure.
Pathological Myopia (M)

A severe form of myopia associated with changes in the eye fundus and an increased risk of retinal detachment.
Other Diseases/Abnormalities (O)

Other diseases or abnormalities that do not fall into the major categories, including rare pathologies.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does your chatbot do?
Our chatbot assists users in the preliminary diagnosis of eye diseases by analyzing fundus (retina) images using artificial intelligence. It does not replace a professional doctor but provides early insights and recommendations.
What eye diseases can the bot detect?
Our AI is trained to recognize the following conditions:
  • Normal (N)
  • Glaucoma (G)
  • Cataract (C)
  • Age-related Macular Degeneration (A)
How do I upload a fundus image?
Users can upload an image of the eye directly from their device (smartphone, tablet, or computer) through the upload form on our website. The system will then analyze the image.
Can I trust the diagnostic results?
Our system is based on the verified ODIR medical dataset and uses convolutional neural networks (CNNs) for analysis. However, the results are preliminary, and only a certified ophthalmologist can make a final diagnosis.
Is the service free?
The basic diagnostic service is free. In the future, premium features may be added, such as:
  • Detailed PDF reports
  • Online consultations with doctors
  • Analysis history storage
