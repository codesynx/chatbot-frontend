Age-related Macular Degeneration (A)

Age-related macular degeneration (AMD) is a retinal disease that affects the macula (the central part of the retina responsible for sharp central vision). It is one of the leading causes of vision loss in people over 50 years old.

Types of AMD

Early Stage (Drusen)

- YDrusen are yellowish deposits under the retina composed of proteins and lipids.
- In fundus analysis, they appear as small or large bright spots in the macular area.
- At this stage, a person may not experience symptoms or may notice slight contrast reduction.

Dry AMD

- Characterized by progressive atrophy of retinal cells.
- Analysis reveals thinning of the retinal pigment epithelium (RPE) layer in the macular zone.
- It progresses slowly but can lead to significant central vision loss.

Wet AMD

- A more aggressive form in which new abnormal blood vessels (neovascularization) develop in the macula.
- These vessels may leak and bleed, causing retinal swelling and rapid vision deterioration.
- Fundus analysis shows dark spots (hemorrhages) and swelling in the macular area.

Analysis Results

  • People over 50 are at higher risk of developing AMD.
  • The analysis identifies drusen, retinal atrophy, neovascularization, and hemorrhages in the macular region.
  • The wet form is less common but progresses much faster than the dry form, which may develop over several years.

What Does AMD Result Mean?

If your analysis result indicates AMD, it means that age-related changes are occurring in the central retinal zone, potentially leading to vision impairment. Early detection is crucial, as the wet form requires immediate treatment.

Recommendations:

  • If diagnosed with AMD, regular ophthalmologist check-ups are essential.
  • Eat foods rich in antioxidants, lutein, and omega-3 fatty acids (fish, spinach, eggs, nuts) to slow AMD progression.
  • Wet AMD may require anti-VEGF injections to suppress abnormal blood vessel growth.
  • Wear UV-protective glasses, as UV rays contribute to retinal degeneration.
  • Control blood pressure and cholesterol levels, as vascular disorders can worsen AMD.
